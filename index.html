Synaptic

AI-powered Google Ads campaign automation for small businesses.

Synaptic helps business owners who have no marketing team. You describe your business in plain words, and Synaptic builds a complete Google Ads Search campaign for you.

What Synaptic does

  • Keyword research: finds relevant search terms for your business.
  • Competitor research: looks at who else is advertising in your market.
  • Negative keywords: filters out searches that would waste your budget.
  • Ad writing: writes Google Search ad headlines and descriptions that follow Google's rules.
  • Campaign setup: builds the budget, targeting, ad groups, keywords and ads. When you connect a Google Ads account and click the push button, it creates the campaign there paused, so you review it before anything goes live.
  • Reports: shows how your campaign performs.

How it uses Google data

If you connect a Google Ads account, Synaptic uses the Google Ads API only to create and manage campaigns and to read reports for that account, with your permission. See our Privacy Policy.
