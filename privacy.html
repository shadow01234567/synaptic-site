Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Synaptic ("we", "us") is an AI tool that helps small business owners create Google Ads Search campaigns. It is built by a team of four university students as a Final Year Project. Website: https://aisnaptic.com. Contact: sashaheer.ali10@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you type in: business name, website, description, city, budget and goals.
  • Google user data, only if you connect a Google Ads account: campaign, ad group, keyword, ad and performance data of the account you authorize. We access it through the Google Ads API and OAuth, only with your permission.

How we use it

  • To research keywords and competitors, write ad copy and build a campaign plan for your business.
  • If you click the push button, to create that campaign (paused by default) in the Google Ads account you authorized.
  • To show you reports about that account's campaigns.

We use Google user data only to provide these features to you. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Who we share it with

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): the business description you type is sent to Anthropic's API to generate keywords, competitor research and ad text. Google Ads account data is not sent to Anthropic.
  • Google: campaign data you choose to push is sent to Google Ads.
  • Hosting providers that run our website and dashboard (Vercel and Streamlit).

We do not sell or rent your information. We may disclose it if the law requires it.

Storage, security and retention

Results are generated for your session. We do not keep a long-term database of your Google Ads data. Authorization tokens are kept only as long as needed to run your request and are stored securely, never in public code.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Synaptic's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask us to delete any information we hold about you, email sashaheer.ali10@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Synaptic's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
